The general normal-attack model applies item IAS diminishing returns, adds known SIAS, subtracts WSM, clamps EIAS to −85…75, and calculates frames using the chosen animation record's frame count, starting frame, and animation speed. Source lines and equations are documented on D2R.org's page. The implementation is labeled `REFERENCE_BASED`; it is not represented as in-game verified. Multiple Shot and Guided Arrow reuse only the Amazon bow/crossbow A1 timing; projectile counts and target behavior are not modeled.

Known risks: D2R.org describes its legacy starting-frame convention, including exceptions for Amazon/Sorceress weapon classes. Broad class/weapon table coverage does not itself prove every item's legal equip mode or every skill's attack sequence. Trap laying is implemented only under its stated single-weapon/BoS scope. Barbarian dual-wield normal timing remains conflicted; several multi-hit skills and skill-speed effects remain separately gated.
